DevOps / Site Reliability Engineer
Kestrel Payments - Dublin, Ireland (or remote within Ireland)

About us
Kestrel Payments processes card payments for 18,000 independent shops, cafes and market stalls. When our platform is down, small businesses can't take money, so reliability is the product. We are 90 people, a third of them engineers, and we have been profitable for three years.

The role
Our platform team of seven keeps 140 services running across three regions. You'll improve how we provision, deploy and observe them, and you'll be the reliability partner for two product squads. Most of our services are written in Java and Python, and our deploy pipeline runs on GitHub Actions. You won't need to write application code, but you'll read plenty of it.

We are halfway through moving the last of our older virtual machines into containers, and we want someone who enjoys finishing migrations as much as starting them. You'll also own our disaster-recovery exercises, which we run every quarter with the whole engineering team watching.

Your day-to-day
- Run and upgrade our production clusters and the networking around them.
- Turn manual runbooks into code and remove toil from on-call.
- Define service-level objectives with product teams and build the alerting that protects them.
- Lead incident reviews that focus on systems, not blame.
- Plan capacity for seasonal peaks such as Black Friday and the Christmas markets.
- Keep our cloud bill honest by finding and removing waste.
- Share what you learn: we run a short internal talk every other Friday.

Must have
- A minimum of 4 years in DevOps, SRE or infrastructure roles.
- Running Kubernetes in production, including upgrades and troubleshooting.
- Infrastructure as code with Terraform.
- Deep, hands-on AWS experience, especially networking, IAM and managed databases.

Preferred
- Packaging applications with Helm.
- Monitoring and alerting with Prometheus.
- GitOps delivery with ArgoCD.
- Some Go, for operators or small internal tools.

Location and benefits
- Office in Dublin's Docklands; fully remote within Ireland also works.
- On-call is paid, and rotations are one week in six.
- Pension matching up to 8%, 25 days of holiday plus public holidays, and a cycle-to-work scheme.
- A yearly budget for conferences, books and certifications.
- A new laptop of your choice, and a home-office allowance in your first month.

Java Backend Engineer
Halden Mutual - Leeds, UK (hybrid)

About us
Halden Mutual insures 900,000 homes and cars across the UK. Our engineering team of 80 is replacing a 20-year-old policy system one service at a time, and we would rather do it carefully than quickly.

The team
You'll join the Claims team, which owns everything from the first notification of loss to the final payout. Our services run on a shared Kubernetes platform looked after by the platform team, and claims handlers work in an internal tool built with Angular. We plan in Jira, but we keep the process light.

Responsibilities
- Design and build services that replace parts of the legacy claims system.
- Own the APIs and events shared with the policy and payments teams.
- Sit with claims handlers to understand their work before changing it.
- Keep services observable, documented and easy to operate.
- Help interview and onboard new engineers as the team grows.

What we're looking for
- 5 or more years of Java development.
- Commercial experience with Spring Boot.
- Experience designing and running microservices.
- A habit of writing tests and readable code.

Nice to have
- Event streaming with Kafka.
- Persistence with Hibernate.
- Building and shipping images with Docker.
- Deploying to Kubernetes.

Benefits
